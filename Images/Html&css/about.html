Special Characters and Comments Example

Special Characters

Here are some special characters:

  • Less than: < (<)
  • Greater than: > (>)
  • Ampersand: & (&)
  • Copyright symbol: © (©)
  • Registered trademark: ® (®)
  • Degree symbol: ° (°)
  • Section symbol: § (§)

Comments in HTML

The above list shows various special characters using HTML entities.
